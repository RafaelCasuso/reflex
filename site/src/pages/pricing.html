What is counted

One unit: a governed action, a tool call your agent made that REFLEX decided about, whatever the verdict and whichever host made it. Reads and writes count the same; a decision served from the cache counts once. You are never billed in model tokens, and the open version counts nothing because it reports to no one.

Where a governed action is counted is still being decided (the decisions are made on your machine; the control plane sees the ones a claimed project uploads). The repository records that decision when it is made, and the meter follows it, never the other way round.

Open and hosted

Open, under Apache-2.0, forever: the hook, the daemon, the policy engine and language, the local record, signed team policies without an account, a semantic provider of your own with your own key. What the plans add comes from the hosted side: the decision timeline across every claimed machine, retention per organization with deletion on request, the audit log, approval learning that proposes rules and never enforces them on its own, team administration, and support.

An organization that would rather run the control plane itself can: it is a PostgreSQL database and one service, and the Enterprise plan includes that deployment.

Questions

Does the free version expire?
No. It is the open-source product, and it needs no account. The Developer plan above is the hosted control plane's free tier, for one person who wants the timeline across machines.
What leaves my machine if I claim a project?
Each decision's summary by name (the tool, the rules that matched, the verdict, the latency) and the redacted record; never an argument value of an action a rule decided, never a path or a working directory. rfx claim shows the full statement and sends nothing before you agree. What is stored, and for how long.
Can we keep the data in our own infrastructure?
Yes, with the Enterprise plan, or by running the open daemon alone, which keeps everything local.
